What that means

Max loss per trade—Bankroll × risk per trade.
Size from your stop—Loss budget ÷ (stop distance + round-trip costs).
Lottery-ticket cap—Playbook rule: a small fixed slice per coin.
Suggested position—The smaller of the two above.
If it goes to zero—A rug skips your stop. This is the real worst case.
Move needed to break even—Costs come off both ends of the trade.

CSV formats you can import
  • Capo journal: the file Export CSV makes. Re-importing skips duplicates.
  • Simple Capo: one row per closed trade, header opened,coin,size,back (optional: closed,fees,risk,mae,mfe,unit,note). date, token/symbol, cost/spent and proceeds/received also work.
  • Exchange trade history (Binance-style spot export): Date(UTC),Pair,Side,Price,Executed,Amount,Fee. Fills are paired into round trips per pair at average cost. Only SOL, USDT, USDC or USD quote pairs.
  • Swap list (any wallet or tracker export renamed to these headers): time,side,token,amount plus sol or usd (what you paid or got). Same average-cost pairing.

How the size is worked out: loss budget = bankroll × risk %. If the stop is hit you lose roughly the stop distance plus round-trip costs, so size = budget ÷ (stop % + costs %). The lottery cap then limits it, because a rug or a gap jumps straight past your stop. Price impact uses the Playbook’s constant-product rule with the SOL side taken as half the shown liquidity. Real pools, routing and other sellers can make it worse. Not financial advice.
