The honest numbers

What our own tests actually showed.

My crew spent two months paper-testing Solana memecoin entry strategies. Every rule was written down before we looked at the results.

Every timing strategy we pre-registered lost money after costs.

−21.2%

Entries filtered for “stayer” traits averaged −21.2% per trade on independent data.

6–9%

Buying right after graduation lost 6–9% per trade.

7–10%

Buying coins as they crossed $100k lost 7–10% per trade.

~4.8–6.9%

Round-trip trading costs were about 4.8–6.9%: 6.9% at 0.02 SOL, 4.8% at 0.50 SOL.

20.7%

Only 20.7% of coins that crossed $100k market cap (73 of 353) were still at or above $100k 24 hours later.

Copying "smart" sniper wallets mostly picked the same hot coins everyone picks. It wasn't insider info.

So we stopped hunting for a magic entry signal. What we kept: avoid rugs, size like a lottery ticket, research every time.

The Capo Playbook is that process, packaged up: the book my crew runs before we touch a coin. It's a research and content toolkit, not a signal service.
