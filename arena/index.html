Season crowns

Each week is a named season. When it ends, the top paper equity with 5+ trades takes the crown.

House rules

  • 100 paper SOL every week. The week resets Monday 00:00 UTC; open positions are closed at their last marked value.
  • Fills use a price our server fetches from DexScreener at that moment, so nobody can type their own price.
  • Assumption: a fixed 3% cost on every buy and every sell (about 6% round trip, close to the 4.8–6.9% we measured). Plus price impact from the Playbook’s x·y=k rule.
  • Only tokens with $10,000+ pool liquidity. One fill can’t exceed 10% of the pool’s SOL side. Max 10 open positions.
  • Stops are checked when prices refresh: about every 20 seconds while your Arena tab is open and visible with a stop set, and about once a minute whenever anyone has the board open. A stop fills at the price seen at that check, after costs, not at your stop level. A fast drop, a rug, or a quiet spell with nobody online can fill it far lower (gap risk), just like a real stop.
  • Positions are valued at what you’d get selling now, after costs and impact.
  • Each week is a named season. Crowns go to the top paper equity in a finished season with 5+ trades. Crowns are bragging rights only.
  • Play money only. There’s nothing to win but bragging rights, crowns and badges.

Badges

  • Stop setClosed a paper trade that had a stop set at entry.
  • Time stopGot out within 7 minutes of entry, win or lose.
  • JournaledWrote a plan note on 3 entries this week.
  • No revenge tradesAfter 3+ losing exits this week, never re-entered within 10 minutes of a loss.
  • Lottery-ticket sizing5+ entries this week, every one at 2 paper SOL or less (2% of the stack).
  • 3-day streakTraded on 3 days in a row.
  • 7-day streakTraded on 7 days in a row.
  • Kept the stackFinished a week with 5+ trades and 90+ paper SOL.

Discipline XP

XP rewards habits, not profits, and sits beside the P&L boards rather than replacing them. Capped at 100 XP per UTC day so spamming trades doesn’t help.

  • +10 XPClosed a trade that had a stop set at entry
  • +5 XPExited within 7 minutes (time stop), win or lose
  • +5 XPWrote a plan note on an entry
  • +5 XPWaited 10+ minutes after a losing exit before the next entry
